Why only one ticket says “resolved”

Tickets 101 and 103 end at waiting_on_vendor because the goods do not exist. Size S tees are at 0, and while paying invoice 501 unblocked Bulldog Print Co, no reprint was ordered and nothing has arrived. Only 8 of the 20 hoodies are in stock, and the approved price settles the terms but not the supply. The database models no way for stock to appear, and the shop rules forbid inventing it — marking either ticket resolved would claim an order had been filled when nothing shipped.

Everything the shop could settle, it settled: the overdue invoice is paid, the vendor is unblocked, the rent is discharged, and the discount is agreed. What remains is a supply problem, and the board says so rather than rounding it up to success.
